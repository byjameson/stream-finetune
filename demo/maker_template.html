Tiny AI Maker

Bring a small labelled text dataset. This page fits a Complement Naive Bayes in your browser, shows held-out accuracy with a confidence interval, the int8 weight size and the example size, and exports a single HTML file, a QR, a favicon, a WAV or a PDF model card. No data leaves the page.

1. Data

CSV/TSV with two columns: text,label. Quote fields that contain commas. JSONL with {"text":"...","label":"..."} also works.

2. Train

The model is the selected examples; the training code is shared. A smaller budget means a smaller artifact and usually a little less accuracy.

Parse some data first.

3. Result

4. QR export

Pack the learned weights into a URL/QR, a 32×32 PNG favicon, or an 8-bit WAV. Small vocabularies fit; the page tries a smaller vocabulary if needed. The viewers are qr.html, favicon.html, and audio.html.

Built with the same 130 KB AI recipe. The artifact is the data; the training procedure is the shared code. Nothing is uploaded.
