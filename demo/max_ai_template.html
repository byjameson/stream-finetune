What is this?

The 130 KB page is the minimum-bit hook: 500 teacher-labelled examples and a Naive Bayes fitted in the browser. This page is the other end of the same trade-off: stored weights, trained on 75,000 human labels, and a 1.8 MB artifact. Both score on the same 400 held-out AG News rows.

The model is a TF-IDF (uni- and bigrams, 75k features) + multinomial logistic regression. Coefficients are int8-quantized; vocabulary and IDF are embedded. The accuracy is computed live in your browser. The test set is not part of the model.
