Demo prompts
============

1. What is the launch code?
2. How long do I have to submit a warranty claim?
3. What class should I book for a four-hour business flight?
4. What is the visitor badge code?
5. What is the population of Paris?   <- no local source; this becomes a gap
6. Propose curiosity topics           <- creates pending research items
7. Add task buy milk
8. My name is Alex
9. What is my name?

The first four use local RAG and cite a source. Prompt 5 should not be answered
from a local source; it is recorded as a knowledge gap. Nothing researches it
until you approve the pending curiosity topic.
