Run a fine-tuned model in your browser

No server, no install, no upload. Pick a GGUF file from your disk and it runs in this tab with WebAssembly. Everything stays on your machine.

idle
Built by stream-finetune. Model loading and inference use wllama (llama.cpp compiled to WASM), fetched from a CDN. A 0.5B Q4 model is ~400 MB and wants ~1 GB of RAM; larger models will be slow or will fail — this page is for models that fit.
